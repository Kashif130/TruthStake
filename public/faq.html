How TruthStake works

1. File a claim

You write a single, checkable factual claim and stake GEN behind it. A dedicated Claim contract is deployed just for that case. You then fund it directly from your wallet — the stake lives in the contract, not with TruthStake.

2. Backers pile on

Anyone who believes the claim is true can back it with their own GEN before it's resolved, growing the pool that shares in the reward if the verdict comes back TRUE.

3. AI validators verify

Anyone can trigger verification. A GenLayer validator "leader" independently researches the claim — reading any submitted sources plus browsing the web itself — and returns TRUE, FALSE, MISLEADING, or UNVERIFIABLE with reasoning. Other validators re-run the same research; the network only accepts the verdict once they reach consensus.

4. Challenge a bad verdict

Disagree with the result? Stake at least as much as the original claim to force a fresh, independent re-verification. If validators reverse the verdict, you win your stake back plus a slash bonus taken from the original stake. If they uphold it, your challenge stake joins the reward pool. Each claim allows a limited number of challenges to prevent endless re-litigation.

5. Collect the payout

Once resolved and any challenge window is closed, the submitter and every backer on the winning (TRUE) side can claim their original stake back plus a proportional share of the reward pool. If the claim was FALSE or MISLEADING, stakes are not returned — they fund the misinformation pool that rewarded challengers.
