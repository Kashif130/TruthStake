Case Intake

File a claim for the record

Stake GEN behind a claim you believe is true. AI validators will independently research it — using the sources you provide plus their own web access — and reach consensus on a verdict.

Your stake is only returned (plus a share of the reward pool) if the claim is verified TRUE. If it's found FALSE or MISLEADING, your stake moves into the misinformation pool that rewards successful challengers and fact-checkers.

State it as a single, checkable factual claim — not an opinion.

Sent on-chain when you fund the claim after deployment — this is the amount you risk.
